Setup Guide

Follow these steps to get your SheetsChat widget running on your website in under 5 minutes.

Step 1: Install & Open

Install the add-on from the Google Workspace Marketplace. Open any Google Sheet, then go to Extensions > SheetsChat > Open Sidebar.

Step 2: Configure AI Provider

In the Setup tab, select your preferred AI provider (OpenAI, Gemini, etc.) and paste your API key. Click Save Configuration.

Step 3: Deploy Widget

Go to the Deploy tab. Click Generate Embed Code, then copy the script tag and paste it into your website's HTML before the closing </body> tag.
