Help Center & FAQ

How do I get an API Key?

SheetsChat supports multiple providers. You can get keys from OpenAI, Google AI Studio, or Anthropic.

Where is my data stored?

All your configuration, knowledge base entries, and chat logs are stored directly in your own Google Sheet. We do not store any of your data on our own servers.

Does the widget work on WordPress?

Yes! You can paste the embed code into a "Custom HTML" block in WordPress or any other website builder like Wix, Squarespace, or Shopify.

How do I capture leads?

Enable "Lead Capture" in the Setup tab. The widget will ask for the visitor's name and email before the chat starts, and save them to a "Leads" sheet in your spreadsheet.

Troubleshooting

The widget isn't appearing on my site.

Make sure you've added your website domain to the "Allowed Widget Domains" list in the Setup tab. Also, ensure you have clicked "Save Configuration" after adding your API key.
