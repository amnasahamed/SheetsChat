Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

What Data We Collect

SheetsChat stores the following data within your own Google Sheets™ spreadsheet:

  • Configuration settings — Provider, model, system prompt, bot name, colors (stored in Google PropertiesService™)
  • Chat logs — Timestamp, session ID, user messages, AI responses, model used, latency (stored in a "ChatbotLogs" sheet)
  • Lead captures — Name, email, phone, source (stored in a "Leads" sheet)
  • Knowledge base entries — Category, question, answer, keywords (stored in a "KnowledgeBase" sheet)
  • API keys — Your AI provider API key (stored securely in Google ScriptProperties™)

How We Store Data

All data is stored within your Google Sheets™ spreadsheet and Google PropertiesService™. We do not operate any external servers or databases. Your data remains within your Google™ account.

Third-Party Services

When you use this add-on, chat messages are sent to your selected AI provider (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini™). These providers process your messages according to their own privacy policies:

  • OpenAI Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

We do not intercept, log, or share your messages with any party other than the AI provider you select.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third parties beyond the AI provider you configure.

Your Rights

  • Delete chat logs by deleting rows from the "ChatbotLogs" sheet
  • Delete leads by deleting rows from the "Leads" sheet
  • Delete knowledge base entries by deleting rows from the "KnowledgeBase" sheet
  • Remove all data by uninstalling the add-on and deleting the associated sheets
  • Reset configuration by using the "Reset to Defaults" button

Widget Visitors

When visitors use the chat widget on your website:

  • Their messages are sent to your configured AI provider to generate responses
  • Conversation history is stored in their browser's sessionStorage (cleared when they close the tab)
  • If lead capture is enabled, their name and email are stored in your "Leads" sheet
  • We do not use cookies or tracking on the widget

Security

API keys are stored in Google ScriptProperties™, which is only accessible by the Apps Script™ runtime — not by users who open the spreadsheet. We implement rate limiting to prevent abuse of the widget endpoint.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the add-on developer through the Google Workspace™ Marketplace listing.
